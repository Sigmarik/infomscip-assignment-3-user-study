\section{Experiment design}

\subsection{Participants}

 We conducted a preliminary power analysis using G*Power with the following assumptions: 
\begin{itemize}
	\item We would perform one-tailed linear regression on the log of words per minute.
	\item Our target power is 0.95, and our target alpha error probability is 0.05.
	\item The null hypothesis would be represented by a slope of 0.
	\item The alternative hypothesis would be a negative slope. 
	\item Our independent variable's SD is $\sqrt[]{1000}$
	\item Our dependent variable's SD was assumed to be 6.834, based on \citep{akpinar2023effect}
\end{itemize}
This resulted in a minimum sample size of 14. 

We conducted a quantitative and qualitative pilot study using 3 external participants.
These participants are P0 (Age 21), P1 (Age 26) and P2 (Age 25) respectively.
Consent to the usage of data was acquired by having the participants fill in a Google Form prior to the experiment.
In addition, the participants were informed that they could withdraw from the study at any point.

\subsection{Environment}

The participants were seated in a small study cubicle with a table and a chair.
Any distraction and noise were minimized. The experiments were conducted individually. 

\subsection{Qualitative Survey}

After participants are done with the experiments, we then record their performance metrics and ask their comfortness level in a google form. By measuring their comfortness level using Likert Scale, this subjective rating can hopefully catches what performance metrics might missed.
They are allowed to comment after the experiment is done, even though it will not be recorded and analyzed.

\subsection{Equipment}

We performed the experiment using a laptop and webcam.
The setup produces a feed of the participant's hands and phone behind the laptop screen, which are otherwise hidden by the laptop. The exact details of our setup are as follows:

\begin{itemize}
	\item A laptop with a 144 Hz refresh rate and 1080p (1920x1080) screen resolution.
	\item A BlueBuilt Ultra HD 4K Webcam that supports 60 FPS with a 1080p resolution.
	\item OBS Studio to create the software setup for reading the webcam feed,
		positioning and scaling it on the screen and applying a visual latency in milliseconds.
	\item The participant's own phone, with any form of auto-correction and spell-assist disabled.
		This ensured that participants were most familiar with their keyboard layout.
\end{itemize}

We shrank the camera feed on the laptop to 44\% of 1080p.
This recreates the normal scale of the hands and phone on the camera feed.
Moreover, positioning the camera feed at the top
of the screen produces the straightest viewing angle from the participant's perspective,
minimizing any visual distortion from the laptop screen.
This does incur a loss in camera feed resolution, but we determined that it did not lead to any reading issues through self-testing.

\subsection{Experiment Instructions}

The participants were asked to open the website MonkeyType \cite{monkeytype} on their phones,
which is a tool to perform typing tests.
The typing test performed was a random 50-word test using the english-1k word list of 1000 most common
words in the English language. The steps to set up the experiment are outlined below:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Ask the participant to disable any auto-correction or assistive typing technologies on their
		phone.
	\item Let the participant position their hands comfortably around the laptop screen while holding the phone.
	\item Adjust the camera and screen tilt to optimally show the participant's hands and phone screen.
	\item Set the visual latency to 0 ms, and let the participant do a test run on MonkeyType to get used to the setup.
	\item If requested by the participant, fine-tune the camera tilt.
\end{enumerate}

After the setup phase was completed, the participants performed a practice round, allowing them to get comfortable with the setup. Then the participant was given 6 typing tests with latencies of
0, 25, 50, 75, 100 and 200ms. The order in which these latencies were tested was randomized to avoid any
learning artefacts. The procedure for each test was as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Configure OBS Studio to use the correct visual latency. The participant was not informed
		of the exact latency test they were given to prevent them from altering their typing behavior.
	\item Verify the MonkeyType test settings on the participants phone and the configured visual latency.
	\item Let the participant perform the typing test in their own time.
	\item Copy the test analytics from the participant's phone to our data collection form.
	\item Ask the user about their comfort level with this particular test for use in the qualitative study.
	\item Take a short break of 30 seconds to let the participants get comfortable.
\end{enumerate}
