Participants' typing performance measurements as displayed by MonkeyType, as well as their perceived comfort level were collected into a table.
We have measured the typing speed in words per minute (WPM), accuracy in percentage, and comfort level on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very uncomfortable and 5 is very comfortable, sample index (the order of appearance), the number of correctly typed characters, the number of incorrectly typed characters and the number of extra characters typed.

To account for participants' typing speed difference, each individual participant's WPM was normalized to their average WPM across all tests.

Participants' average WPM, accuracy and comfort level were plotted against the visual latency in milliseconds, as well as the sample index of the test. The plots are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:results}.
