\subsection{Limitations}

Our experiment setup was subject to many technological and time-based limitations
that impact the reliability of the results. 

\begin{itemize}
	\item The current setup limits the free movement of the participants and can be uncomfortable
	in many situations. This alters the typing behavior of the participant.
	\item While a flat visual latency was programmed in using software, there is still
		a baseline hardware latency that we did not estimate and account for.
		This would require a thorough measurement of the true visual latency of our setup.
	\item Because we allowed participants to user their own phone, there was a situation 
		with participant P2 whose phone was showing popups that interfered with the experiment.
		A substitute phone from the researchers was provided, but it was clear that P2 was not used 
		to it because they were typing slowly with only one hand.
	\item The small number of participants, and the fact that every test was only 
		performed once made most of our data unreliable and susceptible to noise and outliers.
	\item The webcam setup was unstable and could the camera could topple if the participant were to
		bump the laptop or table.
	\item Participants reported during the experiment that they sometimes heavily relied on their muscle memory for typing,
		instead of the visual feed. This feedback could have been included as a question in our qualitative survey.
\end{itemize}
